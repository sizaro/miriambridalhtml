At Miriam Bridal Services, we specialize in providing exceptional hair and makeup services for brides and their bridal parties. Our team is dedicated to ensuring that every bride looks stunning on her special day, with services tailored to meet her individual style and preferences

Our Services

Bridal Hair Styling

Customized wedding hairstyles (updos, braids, curls) to ensure the perfect look for your big day.

More

Bridal Makeup

Custom makeup looks tailored to individual preferences with long-lasting and waterproof options.

More

Mobile Services & On-Site Availability

Bridal party makeup and hairstyling on location (at home or venue), including convenient mobile touch-ups.

More

Custom Bridal Packages

Tailored wedding beauty packages based on individual needs including makeup, hair, and accessory options.

More

Bridal Beauty Prepping

Skincare treatments and facials, pre-wedding body scrubs, and wellness consultations for brides.

More

Bridal Trial & Consultation

Hair and makeup trials before the wedding day, in-depth consultations to plan personalized looks.

More

Bridal Beauty Enhancements

Eyelash extensions, eyebrow shaping, teeth whitening, and nail art to complete your wedding look.

More

Destination Wedding Beauty

Beauty services tailored for destination weddings with travel-ready makeup and hairstyling options.
